## "Give me the runbook for the night"

The Nimbus go live is in ten days. Operations has asked for one document that tells them what to do before, during and after go live, and what to do if things go wrong. They will follow it at two in the morning, tired and under pressure.

```chat
# Message, Monday, 11:15 AM
Operations lead | Operations | I want steps I can follow without calling anyone. Commands, checks, contacts.
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | And a hypercare plan I can staff. Shifts, scripts and escalation.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | We will review it together. I would rather find the gaps now.
```

A go live runbook is a step by step guide for the event. A hypercare plan is the support arrangement for the weeks after. They belong together, because the runbook ends where the hypercare plan begins. Both must be specific, tested and written for the person who will use them.

```key
A runbook is for the tired person at night. Use short steps, exact commands or screens, expected results and a clear next step for both success and failure.
```

## Runbook anatomy

```cards
list | Preconditions | What must be true before starting, with how to check. | Starting with a hidden gap.
workflow | Steps | Numbered, one action each, with owner and expected time. | Long paragraphs with several actions.
check | Verification | The proof after each step. | Assuming success.
undo | Rollback | When to roll back, how and who decides. | Improvising under stress.
phone | Contacts | Names, roles and numbers, plus deputies. | Contacts that are out of date.
```

## A sample step

```accordion
Step 14 | Switch traffic to the new environment. Owner: network engineer. Time: 10 minutes.
Action | Update the traffic rule to send all requests to the new endpoint, using the approved change script.
Expected result | Health check shows all instances healthy and order requests succeeding.
If it fails | Stop. Do not continue. Call the release manager. Rollback decision within 15 minutes.
```

## Hypercare plan

```flow
Staffing | Named people by shift, with deputies
Monitoring | Dashboards, alerts and daily reports
Support | Scripts, escalation and known issues
Fix path | Fast route for urgent changes
Reviews | Daily stand up, weekly review, exit decision
```

## Your decision

During the walkthrough, the operations lead finds that two steps rely on one engineer who is on leave on the night.

```decision
? What do you do?
- Hope the engineer returns in time | A critical step with no cover is a single point of failure.
- Move the go live date immediately | Perhaps too drastic before exploring options.
+ Name a deputy, pair them with the engineer before leave, record the exact steps and test them in a rehearsal, and add a check in the runbook that both are available before the go live decision | The risk is closed with people and evidence, not hope.
= Remove single points of failure with deputies and rehearsal.
```
