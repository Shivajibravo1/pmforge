## Who actually approves the penetration test?

Week three of Lighthouse. The penetration test is booked, and nobody knows who signs it off. You ask in the project channel and get four answers in ten minutes.

```chat
# Project channel, Monday, 3:05 PM
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | I assumed security approves it. They asked for it.
Elena Rossi | CISO | We review the findings. The business owner accepts the residual risk. That is not me.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | I thought IT owned all of this. Who is the business owner?
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | Who tells us if it passes? We need to plan support.
```

Everyone is competent and everyone is right about their own part. Nobody has been told, in writing, who is Responsible, who is Accountable and who only needs to know. That gap is what a stakeholder register and a RACI chart close.

```key
A stakeholder register says who matters and what they need. A RACI says who does, who decides, who advises and who is told, for each deliverable or decision.
```

```ref
CIS | Controls v8, penetration testing control | Penetration testing is a named CIS control, so someone must own its approval. Use it to justify why the RACI names an approver for security tests.
NIST | SP 800-115 technical guide to information security testing and assessment | Cite it when asked how a security test is planned, authorised and reported.
```

## RACI: four letters, one rule

For every row, such as an approval or a deliverable, assign each person exactly one letter.

```cards
users | R, Responsible | Does the work. There can be several. | Everyone assumes someone else is doing it.
flag | A, Accountable | Owns the outcome and signs it off. Exactly one per row. | Two owners means nobody owns it.
book | C, Consulted | Gives input before the decision. Two way. | Skipped, they appear later with a veto.
eye | I, Informed | Told after the decision. One way. | Learns from a rumour and loses trust.
```

| Deliverable or decision | Daniel | Elena | Rahul | Priya | Marcus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Penetration test executed | I | C | R | I | I |
| Findings reviewed | I | R | C | I | I |
| Residual risk accepted | A | C | C | I | I |
| Support runbook | I | C | C | A | R |

Read the row "Residual risk accepted". Daniel is the single A. He is the business owner. That one cell would have ended Monday's argument.

```ref
PMI | Responsibility assignment matrix (RAM), of which RACI is one format | One A per task is the rule. If you can say that and explain why two As cause delay, you pass the usual RACI question.
COBIT | COBIT 2019 uses RACI charts to assign governance and management practices | Shows RACI scales from a project to enterprise governance.
```


## Your decision

Two senior people both ask to be Accountable for the same row, the go-live decision.

```decision
? Elena and Daniel each want the single A for go-live. What do you do?
- Put both as A to keep the peace | Two As means nobody is really accountable. In week 10 each assumes the other will make the call.
- Choose yourself as A, since you run the project | You carry a decision you have no authority to make, and the sponsor feels bypassed.
+ Split the decision: Daniel is A for the business go-live, Elena is A for the security release criteria, and the go-live row requires both to be met | Each owns what they can judge. The criteria are written down, and the decision is traceable.
= One A per decision. When two people truly own different halves, split the row into two decisions rather than blur one.
```

## AI helps with the register, not the judgement

```cards
file | AI drafts the first register | Paste a project description, never confidential names, and ask for typical stakeholder roles and needs. | It invents people and assumptions that look real.
search | AI checks your RACI | Ask it to find rows with no A or two As. | Do not trust it on who actually holds power in your organisation.
lock | Confidential data | Names and views of real colleagues are sensitive. | Pasting them into an unapproved tool is a leak.
```

```ai
Use AI to challenge a RACI for gaps, such as a row with two As. Fill the actual names and powers yourself, because the AI cannot know your organisation's politics.
```

```ref
NIST | AI RMF Govern function | AI can draft the register, but a named person owns each judgement. Say who is accountable for AI assisted work.
```


## Before you submit

```checklist
Every key stakeholder has role, influence, interest, need and engagement plan
Every RACI row has exactly one A
No row is all R and I with no one deciding
Consulted means two way, Informed means one way
Names are fictional or generic if the capstone is based on real work
```
