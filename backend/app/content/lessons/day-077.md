## "Are we compliant?"

A large customer is about to sign a contract with Lighthouse's company. Their procurement team sends a questionnaire with sixty questions about security and privacy. The first line reads: "Please provide your ISO 27001 certificate and latest SOC 2 report."

```chat
# Message, Monday, 11:20 AM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | The customer needs our certificates by Friday. Do we have them?
Elena Rossi | CISO | We have ISO 27001 for the head office, not for the new cloud platform. No SOC 2 yet.
Legal counsel | Legal | And they want to know how we meet data protection law in India and Europe.
```

Compliance is the discipline of showing, with evidence, that you meet the rules that apply to you. Some come from law, some from contracts and some are voluntary standards. A project manager does not need to be a lawyer. You need to know which rules apply, who owns them and what evidence the project must produce.

```key
Compliance is not a badge. It is a set of controls that work, with evidence that they work. Plan for it from the start of the project.
```

## Four frameworks you will meet

```cards
shield | ISO/IEC 27001 | An international standard for an information security management system. Organisations can be certified by an accredited auditor. | The scope of the certificate may not cover your project.
file | SOC 2 | A report by an independent auditor, based on AICPA criteria such as security and availability. Type I looks at design at a point in time, Type II looks at operation over a period. | Treating a Type I as proof of ongoing operation.
globe | GDPR | European Union law on personal data, with rights for individuals and duties on organisations that process the data of people in the EU. | Assuming it applies only to companies based in Europe.
flag | India DPDP Act 2023 | India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, covering consent, duties of data fiduciaries and a Data Protection Board. | Missing that rules and timelines are being phased in.
```

Verify current details, versions and dates in the primary sources, since standards are revised and laws are implemented in stages.

```ref
ISO | ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system | Certifiable standard with an Annex A control set. Know it is organisation scoped, not a product badge.
AICPA | SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy | A report from an independent auditor on a service organisation. Ask for type and period covered.
```


## What they have in common

```flow
Scope | Which systems, data and people are covered
Risk | Identify and assess the risks
Controls | Policies and technical measures to treat risk
Evidence | Logs, records, reviews and approvals
Audit | Independent check and corrective actions
Improve | Review and update on a cycle
```

## What a PM has to plan

```accordion
Scope decisions | Is the new cloud platform inside the certificate or the audit scope. If not, plan the extension.
Evidence by design | Build logging, access reviews and change records into the delivery process so evidence is a by product.
Data protection | Know which personal data you process, why, where it is stored and who has access. Plan for consent, access requests and breach notification.
Vendors | Your suppliers must meet your obligations. Check their reports and contracts.
Time | Audits and certification take months. Do not promise dates you do not control.
```

## Personal data basics

```cards
list | Purpose and minimisation | Collect only what you need for a stated purpose. | Collecting everything just in case.
user | Rights | People can ask what you hold, correct it or ask for deletion, subject to the law. | No process to answer requests.
alert | Breach duties | Notify regulators and individuals within the time the law requires. | Not knowing who to call at midnight.
globe | Cross border transfers | Rules apply when data moves between countries. | Cloud regions chosen without legal review.
```

```ref
India | Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, roles of Data Fiduciary and Data Principal | If data about people in India is processed, check obligations with your legal team. This is not legal advice.
EU | GDPR, personal data principles and breach notification | Ask legal whether it applies and how quickly a breach must be reported.
```


## Your decision

The customer insists on a SOC 2 report. You do not have one, and a first audit will take many months.

```decision
? What do you tell them?
- Say we are compliant and send the ISO certificate | The scope does not cover the platform, and this misleads a customer and puts a contract at risk.
- Withdraw from the deal | You lose the opportunity without exploring alternatives.
+ Be accurate about what is in scope today, share available evidence such as the security questionnaire answers, policies and penetration test summary under NDA, and give a dated plan for the extension and SOC 2 audit with the customer's agreement on interim controls | Honest, useful and keeps the deal alive.
= Be exact about scope and honest about dates. Offer evidence and a plan.
```

## Where to get authoritative help

Your legal and privacy teams and your auditors decide what applies. Use your own expert advice, and use the project to deliver what they require. This lesson is general education and not legal advice.

## AI and compliance

```cards
file | Records of processing | Include AI features, data used and providers. | Features launched without privacy review.
user | Automated decisions | Some laws give people rights about decisions made by automated means. | Decisions about people with no human review route.
globe | Provider terms | Where prompts are processed and stored matters. | Personal data sent to a region or provider without a legal basis.
```

```ai
Shift | Each AI feature needs a data protection assessment because it processes personal data in new ways.
Example (illustrative) | A team plans to send customer messages to a model. The assessment finds no retention rule, so the team adds redaction and sets a retention period.
Use AI to | Draft the assessment and map data flows.
Keep human | Legal judgement and sign off by the right owner.
Risk | Personal data sent to a model with no legal basis or retention rule.
Control | Record what personal data goes in, where it is processed, how long it is kept, the legal basis and the route to human review. Keep it with project evidence.
Interview line | For each AI feature I run a data protection assessment: what personal data goes in, where it is processed, retention, legal basis and human review.
```

```ref
ISO | ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system | Some clients ask for it alongside ISO 27001 for AI services.
```


## Check your understanding

```quiz
? What is the difference between a SOC 2 Type I and Type II report?
- Type I is for small companies and Type II for large ones
+ Type I assesses the design of controls at a point in time, Type II assesses their operation over a period
- Type II is only about privacy
= Type II covers how controls operated over a period, which is why customers often ask for it.
```
