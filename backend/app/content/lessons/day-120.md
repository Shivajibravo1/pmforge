## The final assessment

It is the last day. Lighthouse has become a programme with a governed AI service, a secure cloud platform and a team that has learned how to work. The board has voted to fund the next stage, and Victoria has one more request: a final submission that shows how you would lead the whole thing, and what you have learned.

```chat
# Message, Tuesday, 9:00 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | Submit your plan, your evidence and a short reflection. I want to see that you can lead from the first idea to a governed service in production.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | And tell us what you would do differently, honestly.
Elena Rossi | CISO | And show me where risk is managed, not only listed.
```

This is your capstone submission and final assessment. It brings together the programme plan, the deck, the evidence of your work and a reflective note. It is reviewed by a mentor against a rubric. On approval, you receive your final certificate.

```key
The assessment rewards coherence, evidence and honest judgement. Make clear choices, show your reasoning and say what you would change.
```

## What the submission includes

```cards
file | Programme plan | Your six to ten page plan, revised after feedback. | Documents that disagree with each other.
chart | Presentation deck | Core deck and appendix. | A deck that repeats the document.
check | Evidence index | A list of the artifacts that prove your claims, with links. | Claims with no proof.
heart | Reflection | What you learned, what you would change, what you will do next. | A summary of the course.
```

## What the rubric looks at

```accordion
Strategy and value | Clear goals, measures, a balanced portfolio and honest benefits.
Delivery and operations | Appropriate methods, release and rollback, service management and monitoring.
Security, compliance and AI risk | Threats, controls, legal mapping, evaluation and governance.
People and leadership | Stakeholders, adoption, conflict, escalation and communication.
Judgement and honesty | Trade-offs, uncertainty and lessons.
Quality of work | Coherence, clarity, evidence and professionalism.
```

```ref
NIST | AI RMF | Show the four functions in your submission.
ISO | ISO/IEC 42001 | Name it as the certifiable management system standard for AI.
```

## Your decision

You notice, on the last day, that one of your earlier artifacts contains an error that changes a number in your plan.

```decision
? What do you do?
- Leave it, since the submission is almost due | The error flows through the plan, and the reviewer may find it.
- Hide the artifact from the evidence index | You remove the evidence but not the error.
+ Correct the artifact and the plan, note the correction briefly in the reflection with what caused it and what check you will use in future | You show accuracy and the professional habit of correcting errors openly.
= Fix errors, say so, and describe the check that would have caught it.
```

## Writing the reflection

```flow
Journey | Where you started and what changed
Strongest work | The artifact you are proudest of, and why
Hardest lesson | A mistake or surprise, and what you learned
Next steps | What you will do in the next ninety days
Commitments | Three habits you will keep
```
