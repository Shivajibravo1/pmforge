## One link for the whole story

By week fourteen, Daniel has had enough of asking for numbers. He wants to see the project without sending a message.

```chat
# Message, Friday, 10:30 AM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | I do not want a report. I want a link I can open on Sunday evening that tells me whether to worry on Monday.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | Then make it tell the truth and make it simple. Please not twenty charts.
Marcus Webb | Business analyst | Make sure it uses the same story points we use. Otherwise we will argue about the definitions.
```

A dashboard is not decoration. It is a small set of measures that answer specific questions for a specific audience. Too few, and people ask for reports. Too many, and nobody can tell which matter.

```key
A good delivery dashboard answers four questions: are we on track, where is work stuck, what is the quality, and what is at risk. Each tile has an owner and a reason to exist.
```

## Choosing tiles

```cards
chart | Progress | Sprint burndown and release forecast. | A pretty line that nobody checks.
workflow | Flow | Cumulative flow diagram and cycle time. | Hidden bottlenecks.
check | Quality | Open defects by severity and the trend. | Defects counted but not aged.
alert | Risk | Top risks and blocked items. | Risks only in a spreadsheet.
```

```ref
DORA | Four key delivery metrics | Choose tiles that answer questions leaders ask. A dashboard of vanity counts invites the wrong decisions.
```


## A layout that works

```arch
# Delivery dashboard for the sponsor
Headline | Overall status, next milestone, days to go-live
Progress | Release forecast, Sprint burndown, Velocity
Flow | Cumulative flow, Cycle time, Blocked items
Quality | Open defects by severity, Defect trend
~Risk | Top risks, Dependencies, Decisions needed
```

## Building it

```flow
Choose the questions | What does each audience need
Choose the measures | One or two per question
Check the data | Fields filled in, states agreed
Build the view | Gadgets or widgets in the tool
Review with users | Remove what nobody uses
```

## Data quality: the unglamorous part

A dashboard is only as good as the data behind it. If half the tickets have no estimate, or states are used differently by different people, the charts mislead.

```accordion
Estimates missing | Velocity and forecasts are wrong. Agree that nothing enters a sprint without an estimate.
Inconsistent states | A ticket marked Done before testing makes quality tiles look better than they are. Agree the definition of Done in the tool.
Old tickets | Stale items inflate the backlog and distort the forecast. Close or rewrite them monthly.
```

```ref
ISO | ISO/IEC 25012 data quality model | Dashboards are only as good as the data. Check accuracy, completeness and timeliness of tracker fields.
```


## Your decision

Daniel asks for a tile showing "developer productivity" ranking each person by tickets closed.

```decision
? What do you do?
- Add it, because the sponsor asked | You create a competition for easy tickets, quality drops and trust in the team falls.
- Refuse and say metrics are never useful for people | Unhelpful. Daniel has a real concern about performance.
+ Explain the risk, offer team level tiles on flow and predictability, and offer to discuss individual performance with managers through other channels | His real concern is addressed without damaging the team.
= Measure the system, not individuals. Offer an alternative that answers the underlying concern.
```

## AI in dashboards

```cards
spark | AI summary tile | A plain language paragraph about the sprint. | A confident summary of bad data.
search | Natural language questions | Ask the tool for a chart in words. | A chart that answers a slightly different question.
lock | Data permissions | Dashboards can expose confidential data. | Sharing with the wrong audience.
```

```ai
Keep human | Choosing the measures that matter and acting on them.
```



## Before you submit

```checklist
Each tile answers a named question for a named audience
Four sections: progress, flow, quality and risk
Definitions of Done and estimates are consistent in the data
No individual ranking tiles
One tile for AI quality if the capstone includes AI
The sponsor can read the headline in ten seconds
```
