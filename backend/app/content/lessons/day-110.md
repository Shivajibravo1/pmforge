## "Legal and security say no"

It is Wednesday evening. The Lighthouse assistant pilot is meant to widen on Friday to all customers. At 6:15 PM, Legal and the CISO send a joint note: they will not approve the launch in its current form. The sponsor calls you before reading it.

This is a simulation. Each choice has consequences. The best answer treats the block as information, not an obstacle.

```chat
# Call, Wednesday, 6:30 PM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | They are blocking me two days before launch. The campaign is booked. What do I do?
Legal counsel | Legal | The assistant can state refund terms. We have not agreed that those statements bind us, and there is no handover for disputes.
Elena Rossi | CISO | One test still leaks order status across accounts. And the logging holds full personal data without a retention rule.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | Two are fixable by Friday. The retention rule needs a policy decision.
```

## What they are saying

```rag
Refund statements | R | Binding risk, no handover, no legal review of wording
Cross account leak | R | Failed security test
Log retention | R | No agreed rule for personal data
Disclosure to users | A | Draft text not approved
Evaluation results | G | Meet thresholds on most topics
```

```key
A block from Legal or Security is a decision input from the risk owners. Your job is to understand the concerns, find what can be fixed, and bring the sponsor a plan with options.
```

```ref
NIST | CSF 2.0 Govern function | Security and legal blocks are governance signals. Treat them as inputs, not obstacles.
```


## Options

```flow
Override | Launch anyway, with the sponsor accepting the risk
Delay | Move the date until all items are closed
Reduce scope | Launch without refunds, with handover, after fixing the leak
Phased | Limited audience first, widen after items close
```

## Your decision

```decision
? What do you recommend to Daniel?
- Override them, since the sponsor owns the launch | The sponsor cannot accept legal and security risks that belong to other owners, and a leak could become a reportable incident.
- Accept the delay without any attempt to find a route | The campaign is lost, and you learn nothing about what could be done.
+ Meet Legal and the CISO tonight, agree which items are blocking, fix the leak and the retention rule, remove refund statements by routing them to staff, approve the disclosure text, run the tests again, and propose a limited launch on Friday that widens when evidence is complete | You respect the risk owners, meet the date in a meaningful way and reduce risk quickly.
= Treat blockers as requirements. Reduce the scope to what is safe and show a credible route.
```

## What if Legal and Security still say no

```accordion
Understand | Ask which items remain, and what would close them.
Escalate | If there is real disagreement on risk acceptance, take it to the governance forum that owns risk, not around it.
Record | Document the position of each party.
Wait | If the risk is unacceptable, delay and explain, because a delay is cheaper than an incident.
```

## Lessons

```cards

```

```incident
# Launch blocked by legal and security (illustrative)
What happened | Legal and the CISO each refused to approve an AI launch. Their notes were short and the team read them as an obstacle.
Impact | The launch date was at risk and relationships were strained.
Root cause | Concerns were not specific, the team had not engaged early, and nobody had listed what would satisfy each party.
Resolution | The PM met each reviewer, turned concerns into a list with owners and conditions, offered a limited launch with extra controls and agreed a decision path with the sponsor.
PM move | Turn a block into a list of conditions with owners and dates.
```

```answer
? Legal and the CISO block the launch. What do you do?
- Escalate to override them | It damages trust and may create real risk.
- Wait until they relent | Delay without action helps no one.
+ I ask each reviewer for specific concerns and what would satisfy them, list conditions with owners and dates, and look at options such as a limited launch, extra controls or a delayed date. I show the sponsor the facts and options, agree who decides on residual risk, and record the decision. I do not override a control without the accountable owner.
= Ask for specifics.
= Conditions with owners.
= Accountable owner decides.
```
