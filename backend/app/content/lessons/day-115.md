## "Bring me the whole plan"

You have learned to plan, deliver, lead, secure, operate and govern. Now Victoria gives you one last brief. The board has approved an AI programme for the next year. They want a programme plan that shows the initiatives, the foundations, the governance, the people, the money and the risks. They will read the summary and challenge the detail.

```chat
# Message, Monday, 9:00 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | Bring me the plan for the whole AI programme. I want to see that you can hold it together: value, delivery, security, governance and people.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | And I want to know what could stop us, and what we do about it.
Elena Rossi | CISO | And show me where my team is involved and when.
Finance partner | Finance | And a budget with ranges and a stopping rule.
```

The capstone programme plan is the main deliverable of the whole course. It joins what you have built: scope, roadmap, delivery approach, cost model, security, compliance, change, governance and operations. It does not need to be long. It needs to be coherent. A reader should be able to follow a thread from the business goal to the control and the evidence.

```key
A programme plan is a coherent set of choices with reasons. Every part should connect to a goal, an owner, a measure and a risk.
```

## Structure

```cards
target | Purpose and scope | Business goals, in scope and out of scope, success measures. | Goals that cannot be measured.
workflow | Initiatives and roadmap | Portfolio, sequence, dependencies, stage gates. | Everything at once.
layers | Delivery approach | Methods, teams, vendors, environments and release strategy. | Frameworks without reasons.
shield | Security and compliance | Threat model, controls, legal mapping, evidence. | Added at the end.
scale | AI governance | Roles, risk tiers, evaluation, monitoring, incidents. | Policy without practice.
users | People and change | Stakeholders, adoption, skills, communications. | Technology only.
coins | Budget and benefits | Costs, ranges, benefits, stop rules. | One number with no assumptions.
alert | Risks and assumptions | Top risks, owners, responses, key assumptions. | Generic lists.
```

```ref
PMI | PMBOK Guide, integrated management plan | Show one thread from scope to cost to risk to governance. Check consistency across sections.
```


## The thread

```flow
Goal | A business outcome with a measure
Initiative | The AI use cases that serve it
Approach | How you deliver, release and operate
Control | Security, governance and legal measures
Evidence | Tests, approvals and monitoring
Value | Benefits measured against cost
```

## Your decision

You have more material than space. You can include everything, in small text, or choose.

```decision
? How do you decide what goes in?
- Include every detail in small text, to show thoroughness | The board cannot find the decisions, and the plan reads as a pile.
- Include only high level slogans | The plan cannot be challenged, so it will not be trusted.
+ Put decisions, choices and evidence in the main plan, move detailed registers and templates to appendices with links, and use the executive summary to guide readers to the parts they need | Readable at the top, and defensible underneath.
= Layer the plan. Decisions on top, evidence below, detail in appendices.
```

## Test with three readers

```accordion
The sponsor | Can they see the value, the ask and what could go wrong in five minutes.
The sceptic | Can they find the weakest assumption, and see how you handle it.
The auditor | Can they follow a risk from the register to the control to the evidence.
```
