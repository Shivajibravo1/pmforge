## Buffer day: polish the portfolio

There is no new material today. Your capstone plan, presentation and deck are drafted. You have a day to close gaps and to turn a set of exercises into a portfolio that you can show to an employer, a client or a board.

```chat
# Message, Monday, 8:30 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | Before you present, take a day to tidy. Check that the documents agree with each other, and that nothing is left half finished.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | A reader should find your best work in two minutes. Make that easy.
```

A portfolio is evidence of your thinking, not a pile of files. Select the pieces that show judgement, make them consistent, remove the weak ones and add a short guide that explains what each piece shows. Use the day to check consistency across the whole set.

```key
Quality beats volume. Five strong artifacts with a clear guide are worth more than thirty uneven ones.
```

## Portfolio checklist

```checklist
A one page guide listing each artifact and what it shows
Capstone plan, deck and presentation outline agree on numbers
Risk register, model card and evaluation results agree with the plan
Cost model matches the business case
Security plan and governance pack reference the same controls
Every artifact has a version and date
No confidential or real customer data anywhere
Spelling and formatting checked
```

## Choose your best pieces

```cards
chart | Business case and cost model | Shows value thinking and honesty about uncertainty. | Optimistic numbers with no assumptions.
shield | Threat model and security plan | Shows risk thinking and delivery ability. | A list of generic controls.
scale | Governance pack | Shows accountability and control. | A policy with no evidence.
file | Decision notes | Shows judgement under pressure. | Answers without trade-offs.
```

## Your decision

You have two hours. One artifact is weak, and another strong one needs minor fixes.

```decision
? How do you spend the time?
- Rewrite the weak artifact from scratch | A big job with uncertain payoff, and the strong piece stays unpolished.
- Include everything as it is | The weak piece lowers the impression of the whole.
+ Remove or clearly label the weak artifact as work in progress, polish the strong artifact, check consistency across the set and write the guide page | The portfolio shows your best work and your judgement about quality.
= Curate. Show your best, and be honest about the rest.
```
