## Buffer day: look back at Level 2

There is no crisis on Lighthouse today. The pilot has gone better than expected and the teams are running steadily. You have a day to catch up on what you missed and look at what you have built.

```chat
# Message, Monday, 8:40 AM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | I noticed something this month. The team is arguing less and delivering more. What changed?
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | We wrote things down. Who decides, what we do when it goes wrong, how we talk to the vendor.
```

Daniel noticed the main lesson of Level 2. Delivery improves when the way of working is explicit. Agile, flow, hybrid, scale and vendor skills are only half the picture. The other half is how people lead, argue, hand over and escalate.

```key
Use buffer time to close open work, tidy your portfolio and rehearse the hardest conversations, because those are the ones interviews and real projects test.
```
