## "We have plenty of data"

The support assistant needs to answer from Lighthouse's policy documents, past tickets and order history. The data lead says there is plenty. When the team opens the folders, they find four versions of the returns policy, tickets full of customer names and phone numbers, and order data with missing fields.

```chat
# Data review, Friday, 2:00 PM
Data lead | Data team | We have eight years of tickets and thousands of documents.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | Two returns policies contradict each other. Which is current?
Elena Rossi | CISO | The tickets contain personal data. We cannot feed them to a model without a lawful basis and controls.
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | Nobody owns the knowledge base. Half of it is old.
```

AI projects fail more often from poor data than from poor models. Data readiness assessment is the structured check of whether you have the right data, in good quality, that you may use, with the access, governance and ownership to keep it that way. It is cheaper to find problems here than after the build.

```key
Good enough data is relevant, accurate, current, complete, lawful to use and owned by someone. Assess it before committing to a build.
```

## Dimensions of readiness

```cards
target | Relevance | Does the data actually contain what the task needs. | Plenty of data that does not matter.
check | Quality | Accuracy, completeness, consistency and duplicates. | Contradictions that confuse a model.
clock | Currency | Is it up to date, with a plan to keep it so. | Outdated policies used for answers.
lock | Lawful use and privacy | Consent, purpose, personal data, retention, location. | Training or retrieval on data you may not use.
users | Ownership and governance | Who owns it, who fixes it, who approves changes. | Data nobody maintains.
key | Access control | Who can see what, enforced in the system. | An assistant revealing restricted documents.
```

## Assessment steps

```flow
Inventory | List sources, owners and formats
Profile | Measure completeness, duplicates and errors on a sample
Check rights | Privacy, contracts, licences and security classification
Fit | Test if the data can answer a sample of real questions
Plan | Clean, label, govern and fund the fixes
Decide | Go, go after fixes, or stop
```

## Your decision

The delivery date is fixed, and the team proposes to load every document and ticket into the assistant as they are, and clean up later.

```decision
? What do you recommend?
- Load everything, and clean up later | The assistant will quote contradictions and may expose personal data.
- Stop the project until all data is perfect | You never launch, because data is never perfect.
+ Start with a small curated set of the current, approved documents with an owner for each, remove or anonymise personal data, enforce access rights in retrieval, add the rest in stages and measure answer quality as you grow | A narrow, good start that improves with evidence.
= Begin narrow with data you trust and widen with measurement.
```

## Governance of data

```accordion
Data owner | Accountable for accuracy, access and retention for each source.
Data steward | Maintains day to day quality and handles questions.
Catalogue | Records what exists, where, its sensitivity and its rules.
Change control | Updates to the knowledge base follow a review, since they change the assistant's behaviour.
```

## AI and data

```cards
brain | Garbage in, fluent out | Poor data leads to confident, wrong answers. | Fluency mistaken for accuracy.
shield | Prompt and data leaks | Retrieved documents can carry hidden instructions or restricted content. | Trusting every document equally.
search | AI helps clean data | Detect duplicates, classify and summarise. | Errors introduced silently at scale.
```

```ai
When AI is used to clean or label data, sample and check the results. Record what was changed and by what method, so you can explain the data behind any answer.
```

## What to remember

```checklist
Check relevance, quality, currency, rights, ownership and access

Anonymise or exclude personal data without a lawful basis

Review the knowledge base on a schedule
```
