## "Which framework should we follow?"

The Lighthouse board has asked how the company will manage AI risk. The CISO mentions three frameworks. Legal mentions a law. The service director asks if any of this will slow delivery. Victoria asks you for a plain language map.

```chat
# Board prep, Tuesday, 10:00 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | I do not need the theory. I need to know what to adopt, what it asks of us and who does what.
Elena Rossi | CISO | The NIST AI Risk Management Framework is a good place to start. It is voluntary and practical.
Legal counsel | Legal | Voluntary does not mean ignorable. Customers and regulators are starting to ask for evidence.
```

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework, often called AI RMF, was published by the United States National Institute of Standards and Technology in January 2023. It is voluntary, and it is designed to help organisations manage risks of AI systems and to promote trustworthy AI. NIST has also published a profile for generative AI. Check the NIST website for the current documents and versions.

```key
The AI RMF organises AI risk work into four functions: Govern, Map, Measure and Manage. Govern runs through the other three.
```

## The four functions

```cards
scale | Govern | Policies, roles, accountability, culture and processes for AI risk. | A policy document with no owner.
map | Map | Understand the context, purpose, stakeholders, data and potential impacts of a system. | Starting to build before the context is clear.
chart | Measure | Test, evaluate and track risks and trustworthiness with agreed methods. | Anecdotes instead of measures.
settings | Manage | Prioritise and treat risks, respond to incidents, improve over time. | Risks known and not acted on.
```

```ref
NIST | AI RMF Core: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage | Know each function and one artifact each produces. This is the most requested AI governance answer.
NIST | AI RMF Playbook | Suggested actions for each function. Use it to turn the framework into tasks.
```


## Characteristics of trustworthy AI

```accordion
Valid and reliable | Works as intended and performs consistently.
Safe | Does not cause harm to people or property under defined conditions.
Secure and resilient | Withstands attacks and recovers from failure.
Accountable and transparent | Clear responsibility and information about the system.
Explainable and interpretable | Users can understand how outputs arise at a suitable level.
Privacy enhanced | Respects privacy and data protection principles.
Fair, with harmful bias managed | Considers and reduces unfair outcomes.
```

The list is a summary of the framework's characteristics. Check the official text for exact wording.

```ref
NIST | AI RMF characteristics of trustworthy AI | Valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable, privacy enhanced, fair with harmful bias managed.
```


## In delivery terms

```flow
Govern | Name the owner, the policy, the review board and the escalation
Map | Complete the use case canvas, data review, impact and stakeholder analysis
Measure | Run evaluations, safety tests, bias checks and security tests
Manage | Fix, mitigate, accept with approval, monitor and respond to incidents
```

## Your decision

The CISO proposes to adopt the whole framework in every detail before the next AI project begins. The service director wants to continue and do it later.

```decision
? What do you recommend?
- Adopt every detail first | The programme stalls while you write documents.
- Continue and do governance later | You build risk into the system, and retrofitting is harder.
+ Adopt a lightweight version now: name accountable owners, require a use case canvas and risk assessment at the first gate, define evaluation and monitoring thresholds and an incident path, and mature it with each project | Governance grows with delivery, with no waiting.
= Start light, tie governance to existing gates, and mature it by use.
```

## Proportionate governance

```cards
chart | Risk tiering | Low risk uses need light review, high risk uses need deeper review. | The same heavy process for everything.
users | Clear owners | A person, not a committee, owns each system. | Collective responsibility with no one accountable.
refresh | Continuous | Review after changes and on a schedule. | Reviews only at launch.
```

```ref
ISO | ISO 31000 proportionality | Match controls to risk. A low risk internal tool does not need the same governance as a hiring model.
```
