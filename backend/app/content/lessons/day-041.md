## Agile inside a fixed frame

It is week sixteen. Nimbus runs agile sprints, but it lives inside a company with annual budgets, formal gates and a project management office. Daniel's director calls a meeting.

```chat
# PMO meeting, Wednesday, 3:00 PM
PMO lead | Programme office | I need a baseline, a plan with dates and a monthly gate. I cannot approve a project that says "we will see".
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | We cannot tell you the exact scope in month ten. We will learn as we go.
Elena Rossi | CISO | And I have gates that are not optional.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | I want both. Certainty for the board, flexibility for the team.
```

This is the reality for most enterprise IT. Pure agile and pure waterfall both appear in textbooks. Most projects run something in between, often called hybrid. The skill is to design it on purpose, so the governance gets what it needs, and the team keeps what makes it effective.

```key
Hybrid delivery fixes the envelope, such as budget, date range, gates and outcomes, and leaves the detail inside it flexible. Be explicit about what is fixed and what may change.
```

## What each side needs

```cards
flag | Governance needs | Outcomes, budget, milestones, risk controls and clear approvals. | Being given a promise with no structure behind it.
workflow | Agile teams need | Freedom to order work, learn and adapt inside the envelope. | A detailed upfront plan that is wrong in week four.
```

```ref
PMI | Agile Practice Guide, hybrid approaches and tailoring | Know the common hybrid patterns and when they help. Interview line: fixed governance frame, flexible delivery inside it.
```


## A hybrid model

```arch
# Hybrid model for Nimbus
Governance | Business case, Budget baseline, Stage gates, Steering committee
Release plan | Outcome based roadmap, Release milestones, Risk and dependency plan
Delivery | Two week sprints, Backlog ordered by value and risk, Sprint reviews
~Controls | Security gates, Change control, Definition of Done, Evidence per sprint
```

Read it from the top. Governance sets the frame. The release plan describes outcomes by quarter, not tasks. Delivery works in sprints. Controls cut across all of them, and sprints produce the evidence the gates need.

## What is fixed and what flexes

| Item | Fixed | Flexible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business outcome | Yes | |
| Budget | Yes, with change control | |
| Gate dates | Yes | |
| Order of features | | Yes, set by the Product Owner |
| Detailed scope | | Yes, within the outcome |
| Team working method | | Yes, inside agreed controls |

## Your decision

The PMO lead wants a detailed scope list and dates for every story in the next twelve months.

```decision
? How do you respond?
- Produce the full list, since that is the rule | The list is out of date in a month, and the team spends time defending it instead of delivering.
- Refuse and say agile does not do long term plans | You lose the PMO and your credibility with them.
+ Offer an outcome based roadmap by quarter, a detailed plan for the next two sprints, and a forecast range for the rest, with a monthly update and a change process for the baseline | The PMO gets the structure it needs, and the team keeps flexibility.
= Translate between worlds. Give governance the information it needs in the form it can use, without freezing the details.
```

## Producing evidence for gates

```flow
Sprint | The team delivers an increment
Evidence | Tests, demos, scan results saved per sprint
Gate | The reviewer reads evidence, not a promise
Decision | Proceed, conditions or stop
```

```ref
PMI | PMBOK Guide, phase gates and governance | Gates ask for evidence. Agile outputs such as demos, burnup and risk logs can serve as gate evidence.
```

## AI in a hybrid model

```cards
brain | AI work follows its own cycle | Experiments, evaluation and tuning do not fit neat gates. | A gate that asks for certainty AI cannot give.
check | Define gates by evidence | Gate on the accuracy measured on a test set, human fallback and monitoring. | Gates that ask for a plan only.
```

```ai
Shift | Waterfall governance now meets AI features that need iteration and evidence, so the gate has to ask different questions.
Example (illustrative) | A project in waterfall governance adds an AI feature. The gate pack includes evaluation results and a fallback plan, not just documents.
Use AI to | Draft gate packs, summarise sprint evidence and map agile outputs to gate criteria.
Keep human | Deciding whether the evidence is enough to pass the gate.
Risk | A gate that checks documents only, while the AI behaviour is untested.
Control | Define gate evidence in advance: an agreed accuracy threshold, a documented human fallback, a security review and a monitoring plan.
Interview line | I keep the governance gates and change the evidence for AI features: accuracy threshold, human fallback, security review and monitoring plan.
```

```ref
NIST | AI RMF Govern function | Hybrid governance should say where AI use is approved, reviewed and logged.
```

```cards
cpu | AI engineer | Delivers in iterations and supplies evidence such as evaluation results for each gate. | Gate evidence arrives as a demo.
users | Product Owner | Keeps the backlog aligned with fixed milestones. | Milestones set with no link to the backlog.
shield | AI security consultant | Reviews at gates and in sprints, not only at the end. | One review at the final gate.
wrench | Agent harness engineer | Provides test harness results and logs as gate evidence. The term is not standardised, so confirm the scope. | Evidence collected by hand.
handshake | Forward deployed engineer | Confirms customer readiness at the gates. | Readiness assumed.
```

```incident
# Gate passed on documents, AI untested (illustrative)
What happened | A hybrid project passed its design gate on documents. The AI assistant had been demonstrated but never measured against an accuracy threshold.
Impact | At the build gate the sponsor found the threshold was unmet and the date had to move.
Root cause | Gate criteria listed documents and did not require evaluation evidence for AI features.
Resolution | The PM rewrote gate criteria to include a measured accuracy result, a documented human fallback, a security review and a monitoring plan.
PM move | Define gate evidence for AI features in advance, and let sprints produce it.
```

## Check your understanding

```quiz
? In a hybrid model, which of these is typically fixed?
- The order of stories in each sprint
+ The budget baseline and the gate dates, with change control
- The exact task list for the year
= The envelope is fixed and governed. Detail inside the envelope remains flexible.
```

```comm
# Explain agile inside a waterfall governance model
Audience | A programme office that wants a signed off plan before any build starts
Situation | Your team delivers in sprints, but the governance model asks for fixed phase gates.
Task | Write a short note that shows how sprint reviews can satisfy each gate without changing the team's way of working.
Good looks like | Maps each gate to evidence the team already produces, such as working software and a forecast, and offers a pilot for one gate. Respects the control need rather than arguing about method.
```

```answer
? How do you run agile delivery inside a waterfall governance framework?
- Ignore the governance and work in sprints | The organisation still needs gates, budget control and reporting.
- Force the team to write full documents first | It removes the benefit of iteration.
+ I agree what is fixed, such as budget, milestones and governance gates, and what flexes, such as the detailed scope. I map sprint outputs to gate evidence and report in the language governance uses. For AI features I define evidence in advance: an accuracy threshold, human fallback, security review and monitoring plan. I keep stakeholders informed so gates confirm what they already know.
= Say what is fixed and what flexes.
= Map sprint outputs to gate evidence.
= Add AI evidence.
```

## What to remember

```checklist
Hybrid means fixed envelope and flexible detail
Say what is fixed and what flexes
Governance gets outcomes, budget, dates and evidence
Teams keep ordering and learning inside the envelope
AI features need evidence based gates
```


```mindmap
# Hybrid delivery
Fixed | Budget, milestones, gates, governance
Flexes | Detailed scope, order, solution detail
Evidence | Sprint outputs, mapped to gate criteria
AI gates | Accuracy threshold, fallback, security review, monitoring
```
