## One page for one idea

The steering group has chosen the customer support assistant for a feasibility test. Before any budget moves, Victoria asks for one page that describes the idea clearly, and a short business case she can defend.

```chat
# Message, Thursday, 10:30 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | Give me the use case on one page and the business case on one more. I want to see the problem, the measure, the data, the risks and the cost.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | And what we get if it works, and what we lose if we do nothing.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | Please include what we do not know yet. It will save an argument later.
```

An AI use case canvas is a one page structure that forces clarity. It makes you state the problem, the user, the data, the approach, the measures, the risks and the owner. A business case turns it into numbers and a decision. Together they are the entry ticket to any serious AI work.

```key
If you cannot fill in the canvas, you are not ready to build. Blank boxes are the most useful result, because they show what to find out.
```

## The canvas

```cards
target | Problem and users | Who has the problem, how often and what it costs today. | A problem described as a technology.
chart | Success measures | How you will know it works, with a baseline and a target. | No baseline to compare with.
database | Data | Sources, quality, access rights and sensitivity. | Assuming data is ready.
brain | Approach | Type of AI, build or buy, how it fits the process. | Choosing the tool before the problem.
alert | Risks and controls | What could go wrong, impact, and the controls. | Risks listed with no owner.
users | People and process | Owner, human oversight, training and change. | Technology without adoption.
```

## Business case structure

```flow
Problem | Current cost, delay or risk
Option | The proposed solution and alternatives, including doing nothing
Benefits | Measured, with assumptions
Costs | One time and running, including AI usage and oversight
Risks | And how they will be managed
Decision | What is asked, and the gates
```

## Benefit types

```accordion
Efficiency | Less time per task, more tasks handled. Convert to cost only if time is actually released or redeployed.
Quality | Fewer errors, more consistent answers.
Revenue | Better conversion or retention. Be careful with attribution.
Risk | Fewer incidents or compliance failures.
Experience | Customer and staff satisfaction.
```

## Common pitfalls in AI business cases

```cards
alert | Counting time saved as money | Hours saved only become savings if staffing changes. | Benefits that never appear in the budget.
coins | Ignoring running costs | Usage, oversight, monitoring and retraining continue. | A case that works only in year one.
chart | Using vendor claims | Results from demos and other sectors may not apply. | Optimism built on borrowed numbers.
```

## Your decision

The model might be able to answer seventy percent of questions alone. The draft case assumes the full seventy percent saving from day one.

```decision
? How do you present the benefit?
- Use seventy percent from day one, because it is the headline | You overstate and set up a failure.
- Use zero until proven | Too cautious to support a pilot budget.
+ Show a range with a conservative, expected and optimistic case, start with the conservative figure, tie the next stage budget to measured results from the pilot and show the assumptions | Honest, defensible and linked to evidence.
= Use ranges, assumptions and staged commitments. Replace the guess with measured data as soon as you can.
```

## Risk summary

```rag
Quality of answers | A | Test on an evaluation set before the pilot
Data access | A | Permissions must be enforced
Staff adoption | A | Involve the service team early
Cost per query | A | Estimate and set a limit
Regulatory | A | Legal review of customer messaging
```

## AI to help write it

```cards
file | AI drafts the canvas | Good first structure. | Statements that sound right and are wrong.
search | AI challenges assumptions | Finds gaps. | Does not know your data.
lock | Confidential data | Use approved tools and do not paste customer or financial data. | Leaks while drafting.
```

```ai
Use AI to challenge the case: what assumption is weakest, what would a sceptical finance partner ask and what evidence would change the decision. Then answer with real data.
```

## Before you submit

```checklist
Problem, users and baseline measure
Success measures with targets
Data sources, quality and access
Approach, with alternatives including doing nothing
Costs one time and running, as ranges
Benefits in conservative, expected and optimistic cases
Risks and controls
Owner, stages and decision gates
```
