## The meetings that eat the week

Four sprints in, Rahul's team is tired of meetings. In the retrospective someone says what many were thinking.

```chat
# Retrospective, Friday, 4:10 PM
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | The daily scrum takes thirty minutes and turns into a status report for Daniel.
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | The review is a slideshow. I never get to say what my team needs.
Marcus Webb | Business analyst | Planning takes four hours because we argue about detail we will not need.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | I only come to the review. If it is boring, I do not come.
```

Scrum events are not rituals to perform. Each has a purpose and a time box. When they fail, the cause is almost always that the purpose was forgotten.

```key
Every Scrum event exists to inspect something and adapt. If nothing is inspected and nothing changes, the event is a meeting, not Scrum.
```

## Each event, its purpose and its trap

| Event | Purpose | Time box for a two week sprint | Common trap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint Planning | Agree the Sprint Goal and the work | Up to 4 hours | Detailed design of every task |
| Daily Scrum | Inspect progress to the goal and adapt the plan | 15 minutes | Status report to a manager |
| Sprint Review | Inspect the increment with stakeholders | Up to 2 hours | A demo of slides, not working software |
| Retrospective | Improve how the team works | Up to 1.5 hours | Complaints with no action |

Time boxes follow typical Scrum Guide maximums, which scale with sprint length. Check the current guide for exact figures.

```ref
Scrum | Scrum Guide, events and timeboxes | Daily Scrum is 15 minutes. Other timeboxes scale with Sprint length. Know the purpose of each event.
```


## Fixing each one

```cards
users | Daily Scrum | Talk to each other about the goal, not to the PM. Take detail offline. | Becomes a report to the boss.
eye | Sprint Review | Show working software and ask for feedback. Invite real users. | A slideshow no one remembers.
workflow | Retrospective | Pick one or two improvements, assign an owner, and check them next time. | The same complaints every sprint.
target | Planning | Decide the goal first, then pull items, then plan only enough detail. | Four hours of estimates for work that will change.
```

## A review that earns attention

```flow
Open with the goal | What we aimed to deliver
Show working software | Real data, not slides
Invite reaction | Ask users what is missing
Update the backlog | Capture feedback live
Look ahead | What is next and why
```

## Your decision

The Daily Scrum has become a report to you. Daniel likes it because he can listen in.

```decision
? How do you change it?
- Keep it, since the sponsor values it | The team stops coordinating with each other, and the meeting becomes theatre.
- Cancel it and rely on messages | Coordination drops, and blockers hide for days.
+ Move status for Daniel into a short written update and the review, and ask the team to run the Daily Scrum around the Sprint Goal and the board, with you as an observer | The team regains its meeting, and Daniel keeps visibility through better channels.
= Protect the team's events, and give stakeholders their own routes to information.
```

## AI and ceremonies

```cards
file | AI takes notes | Minutes and action lists without a human scribe. | Attributes a comment to the wrong person.
search | AI clusters feedback | Groups retrospective comments by theme. | Loses the emotional signal a facilitator would see.
lock | Recording people | Recording meetings has privacy and consent rules. | Recording without agreement.
```

```ai
Shift | AI note takers and summaries are now common in ceremonies, which raises privacy and ownership questions.
Example (illustrative) | A team uses an AI note taker in a retrospective. They tell everyone, review the summary and assign owners to each action before sharing.
Use AI to | Summarise meetings, list actions and prepare retrospective themes.
Keep human | Agreeing actions and owners. Actions need a human who accepts them.
Risk | Recording people without clear consent, and sharing a summary nobody read.
Control | Tell the team, follow privacy rules, read the summary before sharing and confirm owners.
Interview line | If I use AI to summarise a meeting I tell the team, check privacy rules and read the output before sharing.
```
