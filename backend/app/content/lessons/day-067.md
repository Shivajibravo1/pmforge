## "What will it cost, and when?"

The order portal plan has been reviewed. Now Victoria wants the full migration package for the next three applications, with costs that finance can approve and a plan that engineering believes.

```chat
# Message, Monday, 10:00 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | I need a combined plan and cost estimate for the next wave. One document, one decision.
Finance partner | Finance | Separate one time costs from running costs, and show the assumptions.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | And show where we do not know yet. I do not want to promise numbers we cannot hold.
```

A migration plan and a cost estimate belong together. A plan without costs cannot be approved. Costs without a plan cannot be trusted. The key skill is to show honest ranges, with assumptions and confidence levels, instead of one precise number that hides uncertainty.

```key
A good estimate states its assumptions, shows a range, separates one time from running costs and says how it will be updated as you learn.
```

## Cost categories

```cards
users | People | Internal and vendor time for design, migration, testing and support. | Forgetting internal staff time.
cloud | Cloud running costs | Compute, storage, network, licences and support plans. | Estimating from the old data centre.
tool | Tools and licences | Migration tools, monitoring, security tooling. | Overlaps and shelfware.
repeat | Parallel running | Paying for old and new during the transition. | Missing the overlap period.
alert | Contingency | A reserve for the unknown, tied to the risk list. | A hidden pad with no logic.
```

## Building the estimate

```flow
List | Work packages and applications
Size | Effort and run cost per item, using ranges
Assume | Write down what must be true
Risk | Add contingency by confidence level
Review | Check with engineering and finance
```

## Your decision

Finance asks for a single number. Engineering says a single number would be false precision.

```decision
? What do you give them?
- A single number with padding, to keep finance happy | The padding is invisible, and nobody learns what drives the cost.
- Only a range with no recommendation | Finance cannot approve without a figure for the budget.
+ A likely figure for the budget, with the range, the top three assumptions and a stated contingency, and a plan to refine it after the first wave | Finance gets a number, and engineering gets honesty about uncertainty.
= Give a point to budget against, with a range and assumptions. Update it as you learn.
```

## Value and payback

```accordion
Benefits | Exit from the data centre lease, lower operations effort, better resilience and speed.
Costs | One time migration plus running costs, including parallel running.
Payback | When cumulative savings pass cumulative costs. Show the assumptions.
Risks to value | Cost overrun, delay, benefits not realised because old systems stay on.
```

## Risks

```rag
Estimate accuracy | A | Wide ranges for three of five items
Parallel running | A | Duration depends on test results
Benefit realisation | A | Requires decommissioning, with an owner
Contingency | G | Reserve set from the risk list
```

## AI in estimating

```cards
file | AI drafts estimates | Fast structure for the table. | Numbers with no basis.
search | AI checks assumptions | Finds gaps and contradictions. | Plausible but unsupported guesses.
chart | AI scenarios | Models low, likely and high cases. | Pretty output on weak inputs.
```

```ai
Use AI to structure and challenge the estimate, and to explain it in plain language. Do not accept any number from it that you cannot trace to a quote, a calculator output or a team estimate.
```

## Before you submit

```checklist
Scope per application with the chosen strategy
Sequence and dependencies
One time and running costs, in ranges
Assumptions listed and ranked
Contingency with a reason
Benefits and payback with assumptions
Risks with owners
Decision requested, and by when
```
