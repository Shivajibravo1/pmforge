## The Level 3 assessment

Lighthouse is stable. Victoria has a new request: two more programmes, both with cloud migrations, security obligations and AI features. She wants to know if you can lead them from design to operation. The board has asked for a written assessment of your approach.

```chat
# Message, Tuesday, 9:00 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | Show me how you would deliver this on the cloud, secure it, move it into operation and keep it running. I want specifics, not slogans.
Elena Rossi | CISO | And I want to see how you would handle a critical finding two days before launch.
Operations lead | Operations | And what you would hand to me on day one.
```

This assessment draws on Level 3. It tests your judgement about cloud delivery, secure release, service management and operations. Use your capstone and show your reasoning.

```key
A strong answer connects design, delivery, security and operation in one story, makes choices and states trade-offs and shows how the work is verified.
```

## What the assessment looks at

```cards
cloud | Cloud and migration | Service models, shared responsibility, strategy per application, cost control. | Using one strategy for everything.
rocket | Delivery and release | Pipeline, gates, release strategy, rollback and cutover. | A plan with no way back.
shield | Security and compliance | Threat model, DevSecOps checks, zero trust steps, evidence. | Security as a final review.
headset | Operations | Service acceptance, hypercare, incident, problem, change, SLOs. | Handover by email.
brain | AI features | Controls, evaluation, monitoring, fallback and incident path. | Treating AI as a normal feature.
```

## A structure for your answer

```flow
Context | Your capstone, scope and constraints

Operations | Acceptance, hypercare, service levels, incidents

Lessons | What you would change next time
```

## Your decision

You have 900 words and many topics.

```decision
? How do you allocate your space?
- A sentence on each topic | A list shows coverage, but no judgement.
- Mostly about technology and tools | Operations and people questions are missed.
+ Give each area a short paragraph tied to your capstone, spend the most on the trade-offs and the critical finding scenario, and end with what you would change | Breadth and judgement both show.
= Prioritise. Depth belongs where the risk and the trade-offs are.
```

## A strong and a weak sentence

A weak answer says "we will use DevSecOps and monitor the system". A strong answer says "we will block new critical findings in the pipeline, retest the three open critical items before launch, release the assistant to staff and five percent of customers behind a flag and agree stop criteria for wrong answer and complaint rates before we start".

## Your task

Write your Level 3 assessment in 700 to 1000 words using your capstone. Cover the cloud model, strategy and cost control; the delivery pipeline, release strategy, cutover and rollback; the threat model, security controls and compliance evidence; how you would handle a critical vulnerability two days before launch; service acceptance, hypercare, incidents and SLOs; the AI controls and measures; and three things you would do differently. Prioritise and be specific. Submit it for mentor review, and on approval you will receive your Level 3 certificate.
