## The Level 3 assessment

Nimbus is stable. Victoria has a new request: two more programmes, both with cloud migrations, security obligations and AI features. She wants to know if you can lead them from design to operation. The board has asked for a written assessment of your approach.

```chat
# Message, Tuesday, 9:00 AM
Victoria Hale | COO | Show me how you would deliver this on the cloud, secure it, move it into operation and keep it running. I want specifics, not slogans.
Elena Rossi | CISO | And I want to see how you would handle a critical finding two days before launch.
Operations lead | Operations | And what you would hand to me on day one.
```

This assessment draws on Level 3. It tests your judgement about cloud delivery, secure release, service management and operations. Use your capstone and show your reasoning.

```key
A strong answer connects design, delivery, security and operation in one story, makes choices and states trade-offs and shows how the work is verified.
```

## A strong and a weak sentence

A weak answer says "we will use DevSecOps and monitor the system". A strong answer says "we will block new critical findings in the pipeline, retest the three open critical items before launch, release the assistant to staff and five percent of customers behind a flag and agree stop criteria for wrong answer and complaint rates before we start".
