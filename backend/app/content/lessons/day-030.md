## Your Level 1 assessment

Thirty days ago you opened your first Lighthouse chat on a Tuesday morning and did not know where to start. Today the steering committee has asked you for a short written assessment, to show you can run the first phases of an IT project.

```chat
# Message, Tuesday, 9:00 AM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | You have done good work. Before I put you on the next phase I want it in writing. Tell me how you would run the first ninety days of a new IT project, and what you would do differently from the start.
```

This assessment is written, not multiple choice. It draws on everything from the first thirty days, and your answer should apply the ideas to your own capstone.

```key
A strong assessment shows judgement, not recall. Use your capstone to show what you did, what you would do differently and why.
```

## What the assessment looks at

```cards
target | Core competencies | Project types, lifecycle, scope, schedule, cost, risk, quality, communication and change. | Listing terms with no application.
users | People and stakeholders | Stakeholders, RACI and escalation. | Treating people as an afterthought.
eye | Evidence and honesty | Reporting, RAG and decision making under pressure. | Spin instead of facts.
brain | AI awareness | Where AI helps, where it needs checking and what governance it needs. | Treating AI as magic or as a threat.
```

```ref
PMI | PMP exam content outline domains: people, process, business environment | Align your answer to these three areas to show balanced judgement.
```


## How to structure your answer

```flow
Context | Your capstone in two sentences
First thirty days | What you set up and why
Controls | Risk, quality, change and reporting you would run
People | Stakeholders and decisions you would manage
Lessons | Three things you would do differently
```

## Thirty days on one page

A quick map of what you can now do, so you can choose what to emphasise.

| Phase | What you can now do |
| --- | --- |
| The IT project landscape | Name the project type, choose a lifecycle, read an environment path, write a charter |
| Initiation and stakeholders | Map stakeholders, build a RACI, make a business case, write testable requirements |
| Planning | Build a WBS, estimate with ranges, find the critical path, structure a budget |
| Risk, quality and communication | Run a RAID log, set acceptance criteria, report honestly, control change |

## A self check

```rag
Structure | G | Clear sections, each easy to find
Specifics | A | Concrete dates, owners and measures, not generic statements
Honesty | G | Risks and mistakes named, not hidden
Prioritisation | A | Top risks and decisions ranked, not listed equally
```

## Your decision

You have 600 words. You are tempted to cover everything.

```decision
? How do you use the space?
- Cover every topic briefly so nothing is missing | A list of terms with no depth. The reader cannot see your judgement.
- Write only about the topic you know best | Deep, but it leaves gaps the assessment is designed to test.
+ Cover each area in a sentence or two tied to your capstone, then spend the most space on your top three risks or decisions and why | You show breadth and judgement, with evidence.
= Show prioritisation. Senior judgement is knowing what matters most and saying so.
```

## What a strong answer sounds like

Use short, concrete sentences tied to your capstone. For example: "My project is a data migration, so I would name data owners in week one and reconcile record counts before every cutover rehearsal." That one sentence shows the project type, a control and a measure. A weak version would say "I would manage data carefully".

```ref
PMI | Talent Triangle | Pair technical delivery with leadership and business sense when you describe yourself.
NIST | CSF 2.0 six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover | Name them to show you can place security work inside project delivery.
```


## Where AI fits

```ai
Interview line: "AI speeds my drafting and my checking, but I own the facts, the decisions and the accountability. For any AI feature in my project, I set a measurable accuracy standard, a human fallback and monitoring."
```

## Your task

Write your Level 1 assessment in 500 to 800 words, using your capstone. Cover: how you would run the first ninety days; your project type and lifecycle choice; your scope, schedule and cost approach; your top three risks with responses; how you would handle a stakeholder conflict; how you would report status; where you would use AI and how you would control it; and three things you would do differently. Prioritise, and be specific. Submit it for mentor review.
