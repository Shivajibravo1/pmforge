## Your build today: a project charter

```key
A charter is the short document that authorises the project and states what everyone has agreed. It forces the hard questions while they are still cheap.
```

## Scenario

Your company will migrate its public marketing website from an ageing on-premises server to a managed cloud platform. The site serves product information and a contact form.

```stats
0 | Lost form submissions
0 | Broken inbound links
3s | Mobile load target
1 | Agreed cutover window
```

```warn
The site must not lose search ranking, break existing links or lose form submissions during the move.
```

## What a good charter contains

```cards
target | 1. Purpose and business case | Why this project exists, in two or three sentences.
flag | 2. Objectives | Measurable outcomes, such as zero broken inbound links and a page load target.
layers | 3. Scope | What is in and, just as important, what is out.
file | 4. Deliverables | The concrete things you will hand over.
users | 5. Stakeholders and roles | Sponsor, owner, delivery lead, security, operations.
clock | 6. Timeline and milestones | High level, with dates.
money | 7. Budget and assumptions | Summary range and what you assumed.
alert | 8. Risks and constraints | The top few, with owners.
check | 9. Success criteria and approval | How the sponsor will judge completion.
```

## Tips

```tip
Use numbers and dates. Fast is not a requirement; main pages load in under 3 seconds on mobile is. Write assumptions explicitly, because hidden assumptions become late surprises. Keep it to two pages.
```

## Before you submit

```checklist
Business case in two or three sentences
Measurable objectives with numbers
Scope with an explicit out-of-scope list
Named sponsor and owner
Milestones with dates
Assumptions stated openly
Top risks with owners
Success criteria the sponsor can sign
```

## Your task

Write the charter for this scenario using the structure above. Invent realistic specifics (names of roles, dates, budget range) and state them as assumptions. Submit it for mentor review against the rubric.
