## When to go over someone's head

Week thirty five. The vendor has delayed a security patch for the third time. Elena says it must be applied before the pilot. Daniel does not want to cause trouble with a key supplier. Rahul wants you to escalate. You are not sure when.

```chat
# Message, Tuesday, 9:40 AM
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | This is the third time. I think you should call their director.
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | I would rather you handle it quietly. They are an important partner.
Elena Rossi | CISO | I need that patch. If it is not applied, I will report it as a risk to the board.
```

Escalation is one of the most misunderstood skills in project management. Done badly, it looks like blame or a lack of competence. Done well, it is a routine way of getting a decision from someone with the authority to make it, at the right time and with the right information.

```key
Escalate a decision, not a person. Say what you tried, what you need, who must decide and by when. Escalate early enough that the decision-maker still has real options.
```

## Why people avoid it

```cards
alert | Fear of looking weak | You worry that escalating shows you cannot cope. | Problems that grow while you try to look in control.
users | Protecting relationships | You avoid upsetting a colleague or a supplier. | The relationship breaks later, harder.
eye | Hoping it resolves | You wait for the problem to disappear. | A small issue becomes a red project.
```

## When to escalate

```flow
Try | Talk to the person, agree an action and a date
Check | Is it done by the date
Warn | Tell them you will escalate and why
Escalate | Bring the facts and options to the decision maker
Follow up | Confirm the decision and track it
```

```ref
PMI | PMBOK Guide, escalate as a risk response | Escalating is a planned response when the issue is outside the project's authority.
```


## An escalation path

| Level | Who | Typical trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Team lead or counterpart | Issue not resolved in two days |
| 2 | Line manager or delivery director | Impact on milestone, or not resolved in a week |
| 3 | Sponsor or steering committee | Impact on date, budget, scope or compliance |
| 4 | Executive level | Contract dispute, strategic risk |

Triggers are examples. Agree your own in advance, in the project plan, so escalation is a normal step.

## A good escalation message

```accordion
What | The patch has been delayed three times and is needed before the pilot.
Impact | The pilot may move by two weeks, and security cannot approve go live without it.
What we tried | Three requests to the account manager, one call with the delivery lead.
What we need | A confirmed date from the vendor's delivery director.
By when | Friday, so the pilot dates can be confirmed on Monday.
```

## Your decision

Daniel asks you to hold off, because a quiet word with the vendor's friend might work.

```decision
? What do you do?
- Hold off indefinitely to keep the sponsor comfortable | The pilot slips, and Elena reports the risk to the board with your name nowhere in it.
- Escalate over Daniel's head to the vendor director without telling him | He learns it from the vendor, and his trust in you collapses.
+ Tell Daniel you will give the quiet conversation until Thursday, explain the date at which the pilot is affected, and agree that you will escalate formally on Friday if there is no confirmation | You respect his approach, protect the project and set a clear trigger.
= Agree the trigger and the date. Escalation becomes a shared decision rather than a surprise.
```

## What happens when escalation is late

```timeline
Week 1 | First delay, no action | warn
Week 3 | Second delay, informal chat | warn
Week 5 | Third delay, security blocks the pilot | bad
Week 7 | Sponsor learns of the impact for the first time | bad
```

## AI and escalation

```cards
file | AI helps structure the message | A clear what, impact, ask and date. | Overly formal wording that hides urgency.
brain | AI issues also escalate | Wrong answers or leaked data need a defined path. | Everyone assumes someone else will raise it.
```

```ai
Define the escalation path for AI incidents in advance: who is told within an hour, who can switch the assistant off and who speaks to customers.
```
