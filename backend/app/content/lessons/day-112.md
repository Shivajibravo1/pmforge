## From one assistant to a portfolio

The support assistant has proved its value. Within a month, six more AI requests arrive, from HR, finance, sales, operations and two from IT. The CEO wants "an AI strategy" by next quarter. Victoria asks you to turn six requests into a roadmap.

```chat
# Message, Tuesday, 10:10 AM
CEO | Executive | We need an AI strategy. What are we doing, in what order and what will it return?
Victoria Hale | COO | Six requests, one team, limited budget. Show me a roadmap and how we choose.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | We cannot do six at once, and some depend on the same data.
Finance partner | Finance | And I need to see the total spend and the benefits, not six separate cases.
```

An AI roadmap is a time ordered plan of initiatives, tied to business goals, with dependencies, resources, risks and decision gates. Portfolio management is the discipline of choosing and balancing the set of initiatives, so that the total delivers more than the sum of its parts. Both depend on shared foundations such as data, platforms, skills and governance.

```key
A roadmap is a set of choices, with reasons. It shows what comes first, what waits, what we stop and which foundations make the rest cheaper and safer.
```

## Building blocks

```cards
target | Strategy link | Each initiative ties to a business goal and a measure. | Initiatives chosen by enthusiasm.
layers | Foundations | Data platform, security, governance, skills, evaluation tooling. | Every project building its own.
chart | Prioritisation | Value, feasibility, risk, readiness, dependencies. | A list sorted by the loudest voice.
calendar | Sequencing | Order by dependency and learning. | Everything started at once.
users | Capacity | Skills and time of scarce people. | Plans that ignore the team.
```

## Balanced portfolio

```accordion
Quick wins | Low risk, fast benefit, build confidence and learning.
Core bets | Meaningful value, moderate risk, with strong sponsors.
Strategic options | Larger or more uncertain, funded in stages.
Foundations | Platform, data quality, governance, training. These enable the rest.
Stop list | Ideas that are paused or ended, with reasons.
```

## Horizons

```timeline
Next 3 months | Support assistant widened, data foundation, governance pack operating | ok
3 to 6 months | Two more use cases at pilot, evaluation tooling shared | info
6 to 12 months | Scale proven use cases, retire pilots that fail gates | info
Beyond | Strategic options with staged funding | warn
```

## Sequencing logic

```flow
Foundations first | Shared data, security and evaluation save time later
Learn early | Start with lower risk to build skills
Dependencies | Do not start what needs unfinished data
Gates | Fund the next stage on evidence
Review | Re rank quarterly as facts change
```

## Your decision

HR's request is attractive, but it involves screening candidates, and the data is not ready. The CEO is excited about it.

```decision
? How do you place it on the roadmap?
- Put it first, because the CEO likes it | A high risk use with unready data would start in the worst place.
- Remove it quietly | The sponsor feels ignored.
+ Place it later with a clear explanation: classify the use with legal, fix data and governance first, start with a lower risk HR use such as drafting job descriptions with human review, and re rank when the conditions are met | The idea stays alive with a responsible route, and the CEO sees the reasoning.
= Show where it goes, why, and what must be true to move it up.
```

**Measures**

```cards
chart | Value delivered | Measured benefits against the case. | Benefits claimed, never checked.
coins | Cost per initiative | Build and run costs, with trends. | Hidden usage costs.
shield | Risk position | Number and tier of systems, open risks. | Risk invisible at portfolio level.
users | Adoption | Active use and satisfaction. | Launches that nobody uses.
```

**Common failure patterns**

```accordion
Pilot purgatory | Many pilots, none scaled. Set a rule: scale, change or stop at each gate.
Tool sprawl | Every team buys its own tool. Offer a shared, approved platform.
Hero projects | A single large bet with no fallback.
No owners | Roadmap with no accountable person for each line.
```

## AI as a planner's assistant

```cards

users | Human choice | The steering group decides. | Delegating judgement to a model.
```

```ai
Shift | AI can compare roadmap options, but the criteria and weights are decisions.
Example (illustrative) | AI scores roadmap options. The PM changes the weights to reflect a regulatory deadline and records why.
Use AI to | Score options against criteria you set.
Keep human | Choosing the criteria and weights, and recording why. Those choices are the strategy, and the sponsor owns them.
Risk | A tool silently setting your priorities.
Control | State the criteria and weights yourself and record the reasoning of the decision.
Interview line | I set the criteria and weights myself and record the reasoning, so no tool silently sets my priorities.
```
