## Day one of a new team

Lighthouse's second phase brings a new squad together. Four people from three departments and two locations will build the staff tools. They start next Monday, and none of them has worked together before.

```chat
# Message, Wednesday, 10:15 AM
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | Let us not repeat last time. We spent six weeks working out who decides what and when.
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | I would like to be told, in writing, what is expected of my people.
Marcus Webb | Business analyst | A page that we all sign would help.
```

A team charter is a short document that a team writes for itself. It states the purpose, the roles, how the team works, how it decides, how it communicates and what it will do when things go wrong. It takes an hour to write and saves weeks of friction.

```key
A team charter is written by the team, not for it. The act of writing it together matters as much as the document.
```

## What goes in it

```cards
target | Purpose and goals | Why the team exists and what success looks like. | Goals that nobody remembers.
users | Roles and decisions | Who decides what, and who is consulted. | Overlaps and gaps.
workflow | Ways of working | Events, tools, definition of done, working hours. | Differences discovered by accident.
chat | Communication | Channels, response times, meeting norms. | Messages lost in the wrong place.
alert | Conflict and escalation | How disagreement is raised and settled. | Conflict that goes underground.
```

```ref
PMI | Agile Practice Guide, team charter, ground rules and working agreements | Charter covers values, working agreements and ground rules. Know the difference between values and agreements.
```


## Working agreements

```accordion
We start on time and end on time | Respect people's time, especially across time zones.
Disagree openly, commit together | Say it in the meeting, not afterwards.
We write decisions down | Anyone can find out what we decided and why.
We ask for help early | Waiting in silence is more costly than asking.
We do not blame individuals | Failures are system problems first.
```

These are examples. The agreements that matter are the ones your team writes and will keep.

## How to run the session

```flow
Introduce | Each person says what they bring and what they need
Purpose | Agree the team's purpose and measures of success
Agreements | Each person proposes two, the team keeps those that all accept
Decisions | Clarify who decides what
Review | Agree when you will revisit the charter
```

## Your decision

One member says charters are bureaucracy and wants to skip it.

```decision
? How do you respond?
- Insist, since it is a requirement | The team writes something to satisfy you, and nobody uses it.
- Drop it, to avoid conflict | The ambiguity returns as friction later.
+ Ask what has gone wrong on teams they were on before, use those experiences to choose the three agreements that matter most, and keep the charter to one page | The charter solves real problems, and the sceptic helps shape it.
= Sell the charter by the problems it prevents. Keep it short and useful.
```

## Signs the charter is working

```rag

Review | A | First review date agreed, not yet held
```

## AI and the team

```cards
brain | Team rules for AI | What tools are allowed, what data may be shared, who checks the output. | Everyone uses a different tool with different risks.
file | AI drafts the charter | A fast first version. | A team that did not write it does not own it.
```

```ai
Shift | Team charters now need a line on AI use, because teams use it daily.
Example (illustrative) | A team charter says nothing on AI. After someone pastes confidential text into a public tool, the team adds approved tools and a verification rule.
Use AI to | Draft the wording of working agreements and suggest examples the team can react to, then edit them together.
Keep human | Agreeing the rules together. A team follows rules it helped write, and a generated list nobody discussed is soon ignored.
Risk | Unclear rules, so people paste sensitive data into unapproved tools.
Control | Add approved tools, what data may be pasted, how output is verified and who is accountable for the result.
Interview line | My team charter has an AI section: approved tools, what data may be used, how output is verified and who is accountable.
```

## Before you submit

```checklist
Purpose and success measures in a few lines
Roles and decision rights are clear
Five or more working agreements
Communication norms with channels and response times
Conflict and escalation path
A date to review the charter
A short section on AI use
```
