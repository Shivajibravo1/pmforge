## "Can we get a certificate for this?"

A large Lighthouse customer asks a new question in its procurement form: "Do you operate an AI management system certified to ISO/IEC 42001?" The sales director has never heard of it. Victoria wants to know what it is, what it would take and whether it would help win business.

```chat
# Message, Wednesday, 9:40 AM
Sales director | Sales | The customer asks about ISO 42001. Do we have it?
Elena Rossi | CISO | We have ISO 27001 for information security. 42001 is the AI equivalent, a management system standard.
Victoria Hale | COO | What does it take, and what do we get?
```

ISO/IEC 42001 is an international standard for an artificial intelligence management system, published in 2023. Like ISO 27001 for information security, it does not tell you how to build a model. It tells you how to run the organisation that builds or uses AI, so that risks are identified, controlled and improved over time. Organisations can be audited and certified against it. Check the ISO website for the current edition and scope.

```key
An AI management system is the set of policies, roles, processes and records that make responsible AI repeatable. Certification shows an independent auditor has checked that the system exists and works.
```

## What the standard covers

```cards
building | Context and leadership | Understand the organisation, interested parties, scope and top management commitment. | A policy signed and never used.
alert | Risk and impact assessment | Assess risks and the impact of AI systems on individuals and society. | Risk assessment limited to technical failure.
settings | Support and operation | Resources, competence, documentation, controls across the AI lifecycle. | Controls missing for data and third parties.
chart | Performance evaluation | Monitoring, internal audit and management review. | No evidence that controls work.
refresh | Improvement | Corrective action and continual improvement. | The same problems repeating.
```

**Familiar structure**

```flow
Plan | Scope, policy, risk assessment, objectives
Do | Implement controls and processes
Check | Monitor, audit, review
Act | Correct and improve
```

Standards of this type usually follow a plan, do, check, act cycle. If you know ISO 27001, the shape will be familiar, and many organisations integrate the two.

```ref
ISO | ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system | A certifiable standard on how an organisation governs AI. It certifies a management system, not a single product.
```


## How it relates to other things

| Item | Relationship |
| --- | --- |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information security management, often combined with 42001 |
| NIST AI RMF | Voluntary risk framework, useful to implement many 42001 requirements |
| EU AI Act | A law with legal duties, compliance with a standard may help but does not by itself guarantee compliance |
| Sector rules | Financial, health and public sector requirements may add to these |

Check with your legal and compliance teams about what a certificate does and does not prove.

```ref
ISO | ISO/IEC 27001, 23894 and 42005 | Relates to information security, AI risk management and AI impact assessment.
```


## What a project needs to provide

```accordion
Inventory | A list of AI systems, owners, purposes and data.
Assessments | Risk and impact assessments at defined gates.
Controls evidence | Test results, approvals, monitoring records and incident logs.
Roles | Named accountable people and a review board.
Third party management | Supplier due diligence and contract terms for model providers.
Training | Evidence that staff know the policy and their duties.
```

## Your decision

The sales director wants to announce "ISO 42001 compliance" in the proposal, because certification will take months and the deal closes in weeks.

```decision
? What do you advise?
- Claim compliance now, and certify later | A false or misleading claim can break the contract and harm trust.
- Say nothing about AI governance | You lose the chance to show real strength.
+ State accurately what exists today, such as policy, risk assessments and the evaluation process, offer the evidence under confidentiality, share a dated plan for certification and let legal check the wording | Honest and useful, with a credible path.
= Never claim certification you do not hold. Offer evidence and a dated plan.
```

## Implementation roadmap

```timeline
Month 1 | Scope, policy, inventory, gap assessment against the standard | info
Month 2 to 3 | Risk and impact process, controls, roles, training | ok
Month 4 | Internal audit and management review | warn
Month 5 to 6 | Stage one and stage two audits by an accredited body | warn
Ongoing | Surveillance audits and improvement | ok
```

Durations are examples. Plan from your own gap assessment and the certification body's advice.

```ref
ISO | Plan, do, check, act management system cycle | Roadmap items: scope, policy, risk assessment, controls, internal audit, management review.
```

## AI angle

```cards
brain | Inventory first | You cannot govern what you cannot list. Include shadow tools. | Teams using AI tools unknown to the owner.
file | Records | Decision logs, model cards and test results. | Reconstructing evidence for the auditor.
users | Third parties | Model providers are part of your risk. | Treating the provider as a black box.
```

```ai
Start with an inventory of every AI system and tool in use, including those staff use on their own. Add owner, purpose, data, provider and risk level, and review it every quarter.
```
