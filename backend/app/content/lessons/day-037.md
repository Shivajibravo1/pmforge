## Sprint one on one page

After six sprints on Nimbus the team has found its rhythm, and Daniel has asked you to show the programme office a clean plan for the next sprint. It is the first time a stakeholder outside the team will see how you plan. They will judge whether Scrum here is real.

```chat
# Message, Monday, 8:00 AM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | The programme office wants to see one example of a sprint plan. I would like it to be a good one.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | Please do not make it a Gantt chart. We will not follow it.
Elena Rossi | CISO | I want to see where the security work sits inside the sprint.
```

A sprint plan is not a promise carved in stone. It is the team's current best forecast, with a clear goal, a selection of work and a plan to turn it into an increment. Showing it well proves that the team understands what it is trying to achieve.

```key
A good sprint plan has a Sprint Goal that explains the value, a small set of well formed stories, a realistic forecast based on velocity, and a visible plan for the risky items.
```

## What a sprint plan contains

```cards
target | Sprint Goal | One sentence on the value of the sprint, such as: a customer can see the status of their latest order. | A list of tickets with no purpose.
check | Selected stories | Ordered items with estimates and acceptance criteria. | Too many, or unclear.
layers | Task plan | The developers' own breakdown into tasks for the first days. | A manager assigning tasks.
alert | Risks and dependencies | What could block the goal and who is watching. | Surprises on day six.
users | Capacity | Who is available, holidays and other commitments. | Planning at full strength.
```

```ref
Scrum | Scrum Guide, Sprint Planning | A plan has a Sprint Goal, selected items and a plan to deliver them. Use the one page to show all three.
```


## A worked capacity calculation

Illustrative: five developers, a ten working day sprint, with an average velocity of 21 points.

```stats
50 | Person days available (5 x 10)
5 | Days lost to leave and meetings
45 | Effective person days
19 | Points to plan (about 90% of velocity)
```

Calculation: 5 developers x 10 days = 50 person days. Subtract 5 for leave and events to get 45. Because 45 divided by 50 is 90 percent, plan for about 90 percent of the 21 point velocity, which is 18.9, or about 19 points. The figures are illustrative.

## Your decision

Daniel pushes: "Can you stretch to 25 points? The board will be impressed."

```decision
? What do you say?
- Agree, since it is only a few more points | The team misses the goal, morale drops, and next sprint's forecast is distrusted.
- Say it is impossible and end the conversation | Rigid, and it ignores a legitimate wish to show progress.
+ Say the team plans to about 19 points to protect the goal, offer to add a stretch story at the bottom that is only started if the goal is safe, and explain that reliable delivery impresses a board more than a promise | Daniel gets a path to more, the team is protected, and the plan stays honest.
= Plan to a realistic number and give stretch work a clear, optional place. Reliability is the product you sell.
```

```incident
# A sprint with no time for evaluation (illustrative)
What happened | A sprint plan filled capacity with feature stories. The AI story had no time for evaluation or review.
Impact | The story was marked done on a demo, and later answers were found to be unreliable.
Root cause | Capacity was calculated with no allowance for evaluation, review or rework.
Resolution | The team added evaluation and human review to the Definition of Done and reserved capacity in each sprint.
PM move | Plan evaluation and review explicitly, and keep buffer for rework.
```

```answer
? How do you plan the first sprint for a new team?
- Fill the sprint with as many stories as possible | Overcommitting damages trust.
- Wait until the backlog is complete | A perfect backlog never arrives. Start small.
+ I start with a clear sprint goal and a small ordered set of stories, calculate capacity after leave and meetings, agree a Definition of Done and keep a buffer. I make dependencies and risks visible on one page. For AI stories I plan evaluation, human review and rework inside the sprint. After the sprint I use actual velocity to forecast.
= Show a capacity calculation.
= Say buffer.
= Include evaluation work.
```
