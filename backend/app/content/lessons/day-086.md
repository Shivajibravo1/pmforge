## "It is up, but is it working?"

Nimbus's portal shows green on every dashboard. Servers are healthy, the database responds and no alert has fired. Yet the service desk is taking calls: customers say checkout takes a minute and the assistant gives odd answers. Operations insists the service is up.

```chat
# Service review, Monday, 2:00 PM
Operations lead | Operations | Uptime last month was ninety nine point nine. We met the target.
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | Customers say it is slow and failing. Our tickets are up.
Victoria Hale | COO | What exactly did we promise customers, and are we keeping it?
```

Availability is not the same as experience. A service can be up and unusable. Service level language gives you a way to define what good looks like, measure it from the user's point of view and decide what to do when it slips. Three terms matter: SLI, SLO and SLA.

```key
SLI is what you measure, SLO is the target you set for it and SLA is the promise you make to a customer, often with consequences. Observability lets you understand why a measure moves.
```

## Three terms

```cards
chart | SLI, service level indicator | A measurement of behaviour, such as the share of checkouts completed within two seconds. | Measuring server health instead of user experience.
target | SLO, service level objective | The target for an SLI over a period, such as ninety nine percent of checkouts within two seconds over thirty days. | Targets with no link to what users need.
file | SLA, service level agreement | A formal commitment to a customer, often with credits or penalties. | Promising more than the SLO you can meet.
```

Terms are widely used in site reliability engineering. Check the Google SRE book and your own contracts for precise definitions in use at your organisation.

```ref
SRE | Service level indicators, objectives and agreements | An SLI measures, an SLO sets the target, an SLA adds a commitment with consequences.
ITIL | ITIL 4 service level management practice | Set targets by customer outcomes, not by what is easy to measure.
```


## Error budgets

If the SLO is ninety nine point nine percent over a month, the remaining share is the error budget: the amount of unreliability you can spend. While budget remains, teams can release changes at pace. When it is used up, the team pauses new features and works on reliability.

```accordion
Why it helps | It turns an argument about speed versus safety into a shared, measurable decision.
How to use | Agree the policy in advance: what happens at fifty, seventy five and one hundred percent of the budget.
What to avoid | Setting the target at one hundred percent. It is unachievable and unaffordable.
```

```ref
SRE | Error budgets | An error budget is the allowed unreliability. When it is spent, stability work takes priority over new features.
```

## Choosing good indicators

```flow
User journey | Pick what matters to users: login, checkout, search, answer
Measure | Latency, errors, completeness and correctness
Set target | Based on user need and cost
Alert | On budget burn, not on every blip
Review | Monthly, and adjust
```

## Observability

```cards
list | Metrics | Numbers over time, such as latency and error rate. | Too many dashboards with no story.
file | Logs | Records of events with context. | Logs that omit identifiers or hold sensitive data.
git | Traces | The path of a request through services. | Gaps where services do not pass the trace.
```

Monitoring tells you that something is wrong. Observability helps you ask why, even about problems you did not expect.

```ref
Azure | Well-Architected Framework, operational excellence and monitoring | Observability means metrics, logs and traces that explain behaviour, not just uptime.
```


## Your decision

The contract promises ninety nine point nine percent availability. The team wants to add a latency promise, but has no data on how fast checkout really is.

```decision
? What do you recommend?
- Add a bold latency promise to impress the customer | You may commit to something you cannot deliver, with credits attached.
- Refuse to commit to any latency | The customer loses confidence, and users still suffer slow checkouts.
+ Measure latency for three months, set an internal SLO first, and use the data to agree a realistic SLA with a safety margin below the SLO | You commit with evidence and keep headroom.
= Measure, set an internal objective, then promise less than you can achieve.
```

## AI services

```cards
brain | Quality indicators | Share of answers judged correct, escalation rate and complaint rate. | Only measuring latency.
coins | Cost indicators | Cost per resolved query and token use. | Cost surprises.
shield | Safety indicators | Blocked unsafe outputs and policy violations. | No measure of misuse.
```

```ai
Shift | Observability for AI covers correctness, safety and cost, not only uptime.
Example (illustrative) | Dashboards show uptime at one hundred percent while users complain. Sampling shows answers about a new policy are wrong, which leads to a content fix.
Use AI to | Draft dashboard queries, suggest indicators and summarise anomalies for the weekly service review.
Keep human | Sampling and reading real answers. Only a person can say whether an answer is correct, safe and useful.
Risk | Green dashboards while answers are wrong. Automatic metrics can miss quality problems.
Control | Define indicators for correctness, safety and cost, and sample and review answers regularly.
Interview line | For AI I define service indicators for correctness, safety and cost, not only uptime, and I sample answers by hand.
```

```ref
NIST | AI RMF Measure function | AI services need SLIs for quality, safety and cost, not only latency and uptime.
```

```incident
# Up, but wrong (illustrative)
What happened | An AI assistant met its availability target all month. Users complained that answers were out of date.
Impact | The SLA report was green while trust fell.
Root cause | Indicators covered uptime and latency, not freshness or answer quality.
Resolution | The team added quality and freshness indicators with SLOs, an error budget policy and alerts, and reported them with availability.
PM move | Add quality and freshness indicators to AI service SLOs.
```
