## "It is at 80, and we need 95"

It is Thursday afternoon. The Nimbus assistant pilot has been evaluated on a fresh set of questions. The result is eighty percent correct. The agreed launch threshold was ninety five percent. The campaign is booked for the following Monday, and the sponsor wants to launch anyway.

This is a simulation. Each choice has consequences. The best answer weighs evidence, risk and people.

```chat
# Steering meeting, Thursday, 4:00 PM
Daniel Okafor | Sponsor | Eighty percent is good. Most customers get a correct answer. Marketing has spent money. We launch Monday.
Rahul Mehta | Lead developer | The failures are not random. Most are in refunds and delivery exceptions.
Elena Rossi | CISO | One of the wrong answers disclosed another customer's order status in a test.
Legal counsel | Legal | If it gives wrong refund advice, we will be held to it.
Priya Nair | Service desk lead | My team can handle the volume if we know where the weak spots are.
```

## What the evidence shows

```rag

Delivery and policy questions | G | High pass rate

Out of scope refusals | G | Handled well
Cost per answer | G | Within budget
```

```key
A threshold is a decision you made when you were calm. Changing it under pressure needs a reason, an owner and new controls, not a louder voice.
```

## Options

```flow
Launch as planned | Everyone, Monday, with a note about possible errors
Delay | Move the date until the threshold is met
Narrow launch | Release the topics that pass, hand over the rest, limit audience
Pilot only | Keep it with staff and a small share of customers
```

## Your decision

```decision
? What do you recommend?
- Launch to everyone on Monday, since eighty is good enough | You ship a known data disclosure and wrong refund advice, against a threshold you set.
- Delay indefinitely until it reaches ninety five | You protect quality, but give no path and lose momentum.
+ Fix the data disclosure before any release, launch Monday to a limited audience on the topics that meet the threshold, hand refunds and exceptions to staff, publish the date and the criteria for widening, and measure weekly | The campaign date holds in a meaningful way, serious risks are removed and growth depends on evidence.
= Do not lower the bar. Narrow the scope to what meets it, and fix the serious risk first.
```

## Why not simply ship

```cards
alert | Data disclosure | A privacy failure is a serious incident, not a quality issue. | Treating a leak as one wrong answer in twenty.
scale | Refund advice | Customers may rely on it, and the company may be bound by it. | Assuming a disclaimer will protect you.
users | Trust | Early failures set adoption for months. | Winning the date and losing the audience.
```

```ref
PMI | PMBOK Guide, governance and decision making | A sponsor can accept risk. A PM must present the facts and the consequences before they do.
```

